How to tune this

The single highest-value change is roll_regex. The default is deliberately loose, because no specimen card was available when this was built. Once you know the real format — say roll numbers are always two digits, then letters, then four digits — tighten it to ^\d{2}[A-Z]{2,5}\d{4}$ and misreads stop almost entirely: the reader enumerates the plausible readings of each ambiguous character and a tight pattern leaves exactly one survivor.

labels holds the words printed beside each field. If the card says "Admn. No." and that is not listed, add it — label-anchored reads are far more reliable than pattern scans. issuer_tokens are the words that must appear somewhere on the card for it to count as ours.
