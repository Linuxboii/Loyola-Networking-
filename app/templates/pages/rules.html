Community rules

Short, because the long version never gets read.

Do not go after people

Harassment, slurs, pile-ons and sexual content get removed and cost you standing. Several accounts targeting one person in a short window is detected as a pattern, whether or not anyone reports it.

Criticise courses, never people

You can say a course was badly paced or the grading was unclear. You cannot post personal opinions about a named member of staff. Those are flagged automatically and removed. This is the single fastest way to get the whole app banned from campus.

Share your own work

Notes you wrote, past papers the college issued, summaries you made. Not textbook scans, not paid course material, not solution manuals. Anyone can file a takedown and the file comes down while it is reviewed.

Answer honestly

Passing off someone else's answer as yours is plagiarism and costs real points. Fabricated interview experiences and fake placement listings get removed.

One person, one account

One account per roll number, enforced by the database. Running several accounts to vote for yourself does not work and gets all of them actioned.

If you are actioned

You are told what happened and why. You can appeal, and the appeal is read by a different moderator than the one who acted. Every action is recorded in an audit log that moderators cannot edit.

If someone is in trouble

Posts that read as distress are routed to a counsellor contact, not to enforcement. Nothing is removed and nobody is suspended for saying they are struggling. Support resources
