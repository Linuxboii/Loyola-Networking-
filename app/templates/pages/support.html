Trouble verifying

Most failures are the photo, not the card. Worth trying:

  • Fill the frame with the card and keep it flat.
  • Move out of direct light — glare on the laminate hides the print.
  • Steady hands. Blur is the single most common reason a card cannot be read.
  • Front of the card, not the back.

If it read your details wrong, flag it on the confirmation screen rather than trying again — a reviewer can correct it by hand.
