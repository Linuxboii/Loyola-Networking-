Privacy and your data

This app asks for a photo of your ID card. That is a serious thing to hand over, so here is exactly what happens to it, in plain terms.

What we collect

ID card photo
To confirm you are enrolled here.
Selfie
So a reviewer can check the card is yours.
Name, roll number, course, batch
Read off the card. These become your profile.
A device signature
A one-way hash, used only to spot one person running several accounts.
What you post
Posts, answers, votes, endorsements and reports.

We do not ask for your phone number or your email address, and we do not need them.

How long we keep it

Your ID card photo and selfie are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after your verification finishes. Not archived — overwritten and removed. After that we keep only the extracted fields and a one-way hash of the card, which lets us catch the same card being used twice without keeping the card itself.

While they exist, both images are encrypted on disk and readable only by the verification code. Every time a reviewer opens one, we record who opened it and when. Reviewers see a cropped portrait and your details by default; seeing the whole card is a separate, logged action.

Your posts and reputation history stay until you ask us to remove them.

Who can see you

Only verified members of {{ CAMPUS }}. There are no public profiles, nothing is indexed by search engines, and every page — including images — needs you to be signed in. If you would rather not appear in search at all, you can switch that off in your profile settings.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

You consented to this when you created your account, for the stated purpose of campus verification and nothing else. You can withdraw that consent, ask for a copy of what we hold, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete your account from your account settings.

Deletion is handled by a person rather than a button, because answers you wrote may be load-bearing for other students — we will ask whether you want them anonymised or removed outright.

What this app is not

It is not run by the college, and it does not speak for the college. It holds no official record, and nothing here affects your marks, attendance or standing as a student.
