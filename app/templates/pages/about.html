How this works

A closed network for {{ CAMPUS }}, where standing is earned by contributing rather than by being liked.

You rate the work, not the person

There is no button anywhere in this app that rates a human being out of five. You vote on an answer, a project, a set of notes — and the credit goes to whoever made it. That distinction is the whole design. A system where students rank students becomes a popularity contest at best and a weapon at worst.

Your standing shows as a tier — {% for label, threshold in REP_TIERS %}{{ label }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %} — and never as a public number. Only you can see your own total. There is no leaderboard.

The five things that count

Academic help
Answers that got upvoted or accepted, notes people found useful.
Build & ship
Projects with something real behind them — a repo, a live link, a demo.
Community service
Running events, volunteering, mentoring juniors. Verified by check-in, not self-reported.
Participation
Good posts and comments. Decays if the content is later removed.
Trust & conduct
Account age, verification, a clean record. This one can go negative.

Each has a monthly ceiling, so no single habit can carry your whole score, and points sit provisional for 48 hours before they settle.

Why the feed is boring on purpose

It is chronological by default. The "For you" ranking uses recency, your declared interests, your batch and department, and the author's standing. It does not use time-on-page, and there is no metric anywhere in the codebase that rewards keeping you scrolling. This is a campus utility, not an attention product.

Gaming it does not work

You cannot vote for yourself. Repeated votes from the same person on the same author count for progressively less. Reciprocal voting pairs get quietly neutralised. A sudden spike well above your own baseline parks the points for review. None of this gets announced when it triggers.

Every point you have ever gained or lost is itemised in your ledger, with a link to what caused it. If something there looks wrong, dispute it.

What is deliberately missing

No private messages in this version — they are the main harassment route in campus apps and need moderation capacity that does not exist yet. No public profiles, no outside signups, no alumni accounts yet. Pseudonymity exists only in the anonymous doubt box, and even there the system knows who you are, so abuse remains traceable.
